# GEPR-LINAN Source Text Bibliography

Full bibliographic details for all source texts used in the GEPR-LINAN dataset (19 text units in total: 18 IND text units from 15 source works, plus 1 OOD text).
All texts are in the public domain. Digital scans obtained from **Shidian Guji (识典古籍)**, https://www.shidianguji.com — a publicly accessible digital repository for classical Chinese texts.
OCR and manual correction were performed by the authors.

## Copyright Statement

All source texts are in the public domain. The most recent author whose dates are known, Lang Ying (郎瑛), died c. 1566 CE (>450 years ago). All other authors are from the Song Dynasty (12th–13th century CE, >700 years ago). The most recent printed edition used is the 1900 Ding-family Jiahui Hall edition of *Chunyou Lin'an Zhi*, now >124 years old. All works and editions are unambiguously in the public domain under Chinese and international copyright law.

Shidian Guji (识典古籍) has confirmed that the platform does not hold copyright over classical text images, and that the copyright of such images belongs to the public domain. The use of these scans for OCR, text extraction, manual correction, segmentation, and annotation to construct the GEPR-LINAN dataset therefore involves no copyright dispute.

The GEPR-LINAN dataset includes the source texts alongside the annotation layers. All source texts are derived from classical works in the public domain whose authors lived centuries ago. The texts were independently extracted by the authors of this dataset through OCR of digitised page-image scans of historical printed editions, followed by systematic manual correction, paragraph segmentation, and sentence splitting. No separate provision of source texts is required, as the dataset itself contains all source texts used. The dataset and annotation layers are released under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.
